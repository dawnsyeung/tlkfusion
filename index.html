The product

What White Eagle Is Offering

Whole dried black soldier fly larvae in practical bulk formats for the frequent insect-treat buyer.

Positioning

A natural, protein-rich whole-insect feed that makes flock enrichment easy, rewarding, and economical in larger sizes.

White Eagle Nutrition whole dried BSFL help customers reward natural foraging behavior while providing protein, fat, calcium, and naturally occurring chitin in a convenient shelf-stable format. The 5 lb, 10 lb, and 20 lb sizes serve customers who use insect treats regularly and want better value than repeated small-bag purchases.

What it is

  • Whole dried black soldier fly larvae (Hermetia illucens)
  • A shelf-stable alternative to live insects
  • Positioned as a treat, supplement, or enrichment feed—not a complete ration
  • Naturally contains protein, fat, calcium, and chitin
  • Ethically sourced, non-GMO insect protein from a Texas-rooted brand

Who buys it

  • Backyard chicken keepers

  • Owners of medium or large poultry flocks

  • Customers seeking natural enrichment for poultry

Best-fit retail channels

Retailer conversations

Common Questions & Facts

Straight answers for the questions buyers raise most often.

What are BSFL?

BSFL are the larval stage of the black soldier fly, Hermetia illucens. They are used as a whole-insect feed or ingredient for appropriate animal species.

Are these live insects?

No. These products are dried larvae designed for convenient storage and feeding.

How do consumers use them?

Follow package feeding directions. A common enrichment practice is to scatter appropriate portions so poultry can scratch and forage.

Is this a complete feed?

No. Whole dried BSFL are positioned as a treat, supplement, or enrichment feed—not a complete ration—unless a specific product is formulated and labeled as such.

How should it be stored?

Store in a cool, dry place. Keep packaging closed and protected from moisture, heat, pests, and damage—standard practice for dried insect products.

Who is the primary species?

Primary retail focus is backyard poultry. Other insect-eating animals may be appropriate where the final label and channel support that use.

Is it organic?

The line is positioned as a natural whole-insect product. Organic claims apply only if a specific SKU carries organic certification and approved label language.

Where is the brand from?

White Eagle Nutrition is a Texas-rooted brand with a documented White Eagle origin story. Country-of-origin and manufacturing details for each SKU are provided with retail item documentation.

Do you serve retailers?

Yes. White Eagle works with wholesale and retail partners on pricing, samples, staff education, and marketing support. Contact info@WhiteEagleNutrition.com or (713) 497-1745.

“The packages are too large.”

The 5 lb bag is the accessible opening size. The 10 lb and 20 lb formats can sit in e-commerce, ship-to-store, high-volume locations, or special order. Assortment can be tailored by channel instead of forcing every size into every store.

“The retail price is high.”

Compare price per pound and frequency of use, not only the package ring. Frequent users often spend more through repeated small-bag purchases. The line rewards trade-up with better per-pound value as size increases.

“Customers may not understand BSFL.”

The shelf story is simple: a whole dried insect chickens find highly palatable, used for natural foraging enrichment, with protein, fat, calcium, and chitin. White Eagle can support shelf education, digital content, feeding guidance, and staff talking points.

“We need proof that it will sell.”

A controlled test—agreed stores or an online listing, defined placement, basic education, and pre-agreed success measures—is the cleanest path. Review sell-through, conversion, repeat purchase where available, reviews, and returns before expanding.

“We only onboard through a distributor.”

That works. Share the approved distributor, submission process, target landed cost, and any authorization documents needed. White Eagle will evaluate program economics and operational fit.

“Can you private label this?”

White Eagle can evaluate private-label opportunities based on volume, packaging, lead time, compliance, and commercial terms. Opportunities are reviewed case by case.
