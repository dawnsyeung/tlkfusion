Purpose

This thread is the source of truth for TLK Fusion’s retail outreach for White Eagle Nutrition’s whole dried BSFL line. Use the approved language below in buyer outreach and place all questions, retailer feedback, buyer objections, requests for samples, pricing questions, and account-specific opportunities in this thread or the designated Slack channel.

What We Are Selling

White Eagle Nutrition offers whole dried black soldier fly larvae in practical value sizes for backyard poultry owners and other appropriate insect-eating animals:

  • 5 lb bag: Accessible bulk/value size for households with a small-to-midsize flock or customers moving up from small treat bags.
  • 10 lb bag: Larger value format for frequent users and multi-bird households.
  • 20 lb box: Best-value high-capacity format for larger flocks, farms, homesteads, breeders, and high-frequency users.

The line gives retailers a natural, highly palatable animal-feed product with strong shelf differentiation, an easy consumer story, and larger basket-building sizes that are not always well represented in conventional pet and farm retail assortments.

Core Positioning

A natural, protein-rich whole-insect feed that makes flock enrichment easy, rewarding, and economical in larger sizes.

White Eagle Nutrition whole dried BSFL help customers reward natural foraging behavior while providing protein, fat, calcium, and naturally occurring chitin in a convenient shelf-stable format. The 5 lb, 10 lb, and 20 lb sizes are designed for customers who use insect treats regularly and want better value than repeated purchases of small packages.

Primary Retail Consumer

  • Backyard chicken keepers
  • Homesteaders and hobby farmers
  • Customers with medium or large poultry flocks
  • Consumers who regularly buy mealworms or other dried insect treats
  • Shoppers seeking natural enrichment products for poultry
  • Owners of ducks, turkeys, quail, pheasants, and other insect-eating birds, where suitable and consistent with the final label
  • Specialty animal owners whose species can appropriately consume dried BSFL, where consistent with the final label

Best-Fit Retail Channels

  • Farm and ranch retailers
  • Feed and seed stores
  • Independent feed stores
  • Backyard poultry and homesteading retailers
  • Regional hardware and home-improvement retailers with poultry sections
  • Pet specialty retailers with bird, reptile, fish, or exotic-animal assortments
  • Garden centers with farm, homestead, or backyard-chicken departments
  • E-commerce and omnichannel retailers
  • Regional grocery or lifestyle retailers serving rural and suburban backyard-flock households

Buyer-Level Value Proposition

White Eagle Nutrition gives the retailer:

  • A differentiated whole-insect product in value-oriented sizes.
  • A logical trade-up from small bags of mealworms or insect treats.
  • A product that can increase basket size among frequent poultry-treat buyers.
  • A straightforward consumer benefit story: nutrition, enrichment, palatability, convenience, and value.
  • Multiple package sizes that allow good-better-best merchandising.
  • A product suited to repeat purchase because it is used as an ongoing feed supplement or treat.
  • A brand story grounded in regenerative agriculture and circular use of nutrients.
  • Potential educational and promotional support from White Eagle Nutrition.

The Ask

TLK should pursue one or more of the following outcomes with each qualified account:

  1. A buyer meeting or category review.
  2. Submission of the line for new-vendor or new-item consideration.
  3. A regional or limited-store test.
  4. An e-commerce listing.
  5. A seasonal poultry or homesteading placement.
  6. A sample evaluation by the buyer and merchandising team.
  7. A purchase order for the approved assortment.
Immediate Rules for the Sales Team
  • Use only approved product, price, margin, packaging, certification, sourcing, and logistics information.
  • Do not describe the product as organic unless the specific item is certified and the claim is approved.
  • Do not make disease-prevention, treatment, immunity, antimicrobial, longevity, production, egg-laying, feed-conversion, or other performance claims without approved substantiation and label review.
  • Do not promise exclusivity, guaranteed turns, free freight, promotional funds, returns, markdown support, guaranteed lead times, private label, or custom packaging without Dawn’s written approval.
  • Do not quote a wholesale price until the final wholesale schedule, freight policy, MOQ, and retailer program have been approved.
  • Capture every buyer objection and request so the materials can be improved continuously.

Pricing Story for Buyers

The three sizes should create a visible decline in consumer price per pound as package size increases. That gives the buyer a clean trade-up story while preserving an accessible opening price at 5 lb. Final price architecture must also support the retailer’s required gross margin, White Eagle Nutrition’s freight and program costs, and competitive shelf pricing.

Before Quoting Any Account

  • Delivered versus FOB wholesale price.
  • Required retailer margin and any distributor margin.
  • Freight allowance or prepaid threshold.
  • New-store, warehouse, slotting, data, damage, return, and promotional deductions.
  • EDI, portal, compliance, labeling, and chargeback costs.
  • Whether the quoted price applies to direct, distributor, dropship, or marketplace fulfillment.

Section 9

Objection Handling

Click a row to expand the approved response. Copy when you need it in chat or email.

“We already carry mealworms.”

That validates consumer demand for whole-insect poultry treats. White Eagle Nutrition can complement the category with a different insect and a value-size ladder designed for regular users. The key question is whether your customers have an unmet need for larger sizes, improved cost per pound, or broader assortment choice.

“The packages are too large.”

The 5 lb bag gives the assortment an accessible opening size, while the 10 lb and 20 lb formats can be reserved for e-commerce, ship-to-store, high-volume locations, or special order. We can propose a channel-specific assortment instead of forcing every size into every store.

“The retail price is high.”

The relevant comparison is price per pound and frequency of use, not only the package ring. Frequent users may spend more through repeated small-bag purchases. The line is designed to reward trade-up with better per-pound value; final competitive comparisons should use verified current prices and equivalent net weights.

“We do not know whether customers understand BSFL.”

The shelf story is simple: a whole dried insect that chickens find highly palatable, used for natural foraging enrichment, with protein, fat, calcium, and chitin. White Eagle Nutrition can support concise shelf education, digital content, feeding guidance, and staff talking points.

“We need proof that it will sell.”

We recommend a controlled test with agreed stores or an online listing, defined placement, basic education, and pre-agreed success measures. We can review weekly sell-through, conversion, repeat purchase where available, reviews, returns, and customer questions before discussing expansion.

“We need better margin.”

We should model the complete program, including direct versus distributor supply, freight, case pack, promotional requirements, and anticipated deductions. TLK should not commit to a revised price until Dawn approves the full economics.

“We only onboard through a distributor.”

Thank you—that is useful. Please identify the approved distributor, required submission process, target landed cost, and any authorization documents needed. White Eagle Nutrition will evaluate the program economics and operational fit before confirming.

“Can you guarantee supply and lead time?”

We can provide approved production capacity, inventory position, and standard lead-time information for the proposed program. We will not overpromise; the final commitment will be documented against forecast, order cadence, and delivery requirements.

“Can you private label this?”

White Eagle Nutrition can evaluate private-label opportunities based on volume, packaging, lead time, compliance requirements, and commercial terms. No private-label commitment should be made until the opportunity is reviewed and approved in writing.

“Is it made in the United States?”

Use only the confirmed country-of-origin and manufacturing statement for the exact SKU. Do not infer sourcing from the company address or brand story.

“Is it organic?”

Do not describe the product as organic unless the exact SKU has the required certification and approved label language. “Natural” also must be consistent with the final label and regulatory review.

Section 10

Retailer FAQ

What are BSFL?

BSFL are the larval stage of the black soldier fly, Hermetia illucens. They are used as an ingredient or whole-insect feed for appropriate animal species.

Are these live insects?

No. These products contain dried larvae and are designed for convenient storage and feeding.

How should consumers use them?

Consumers should follow the final package feeding directions. A common enrichment practice is to scatter appropriate portions so poultry can scratch and forage, but exact directions must match the approved label.

Are BSFL a complete feed?

Do not position the product as a complete ration unless the final product is specifically formulated, labeled, and approved as such. Whole dried BSFL are generally positioned as a treat, supplement, or enrichment feed in accordance with the label.

What is the shelf life?

TO CONFIRM. Provide only the approved shelf life supported by packaging, stability, and quality records.

How should the product be stored?

TO CONFIRM from final label. Generally, dried insect products should be protected from moisture, heat, pests, and package damage, but TLK must use the precise approved instructions.

Is the package resealable?

TO CONFIRM by SKU.

Where is it produced?

TO CONFIRM by SKU. Use the approved country-of-origin and processing statement.

What quality testing is performed?

TO CONFIRM. White Eagle Nutrition should provide a concise approved summary of supplier qualification, lot testing, certificates of analysis, food/feed safety controls, traceability, and recall readiness.

Does the product require state registrations?

Requirements can vary by state, species, product classification, label, and distribution plan. White Eagle Nutrition will confirm the registrations and approvals applicable to the account’s intended market before launch.

Can the retailer sell it for reptiles, wild birds, fish, or other animals?

Only make species and use claims supported by the final approved label and applicable registrations. Do not expand the intended use informally because an animal may naturally eat insects.

Section 11

Retail Test Proposal

Recommended Test Structure

  • Scope: 10–25 stores, one region, or online-only depending on buyer preference.
  • Duration: 8–12 weeks after all stores are in stock and merchandising is verified.
  • Opening assortment: 5 lb + 20 lb, or all three sizes in poultry-forward locations.
  • Placement: Adjacent to poultry feed, poultry treats, mealworms, flock-care products, or homesteading supplies.
  • Support: Product-page content, feeding/enrichment education, social content, retailer staff brief, and approved imagery.
  • Review cadence: Weekly internal inventory and issue review; buyer checkpoint at weeks 4 and 8.

Proposed Success Measures

  • In-stock rate.
  • Units and dollars sold per store per week.
  • Sell-through by SKU.
  • Gross margin dollars and percentage.
  • Online conversion where available.
  • Repeat purchase where retailer data permits.
  • Ratings, reviews, questions, and return reasons.
  • Percentage of stores meeting the agreed velocity threshold.
  • Evidence of trade-up among the three sizes.

All numeric thresholds should be agreed with the buyer before the test begins.

Market Context for Retail Conversations

Use this as background for discovery—not as unverified White Eagle product claims. Align any numerical or competitive claim with Dawn before external use.

Why the category is open

Backyard poultry and homestead shoppers already buy dried insect treats. Many assortments still skew toward small bags. White Eagle’s 5 / 10 / 20 lb ladder is built for the frequent user who wants better value and fewer rebuys.

What buyers already hear

Competitive dried BSFL brands commonly emphasize calcium versus mealworms, resealable bulk packs, and “chickens love them” palatability. Expect buyers to compare price per pound and package size first.

Where traction starts

Farm/feed independents, poultry-forward regionals, and e-commerce listings are the fastest paths to proof. Use a controlled test or online listing to generate sell-through evidence before national asks.

Competitive landscape note — internal only unless approved

Some retail competitors market claims such as “85× more calcium than mealworms” or egg-shell / molt performance language. Those are not White Eagle claims unless Dawn supplies approved substantiation and label-consistent wording. If a buyer raises them, acknowledge category education, then return to approved benefits: palatability, enrichment, protein/fat/calcium/chitin (as labeled), shelf stability, and value sizes.

Differentiation angles TLK can use (approved framing)

  • Assortment architecture: Clear good-better-best with a true 20 lb best-value format.
  • Retailer merchandising flexibility: Shelf + e-commerce + ship-to-store options by account.
  • Brand story: Documented Texas White Eagle origin and circular agriculture narrative (animals + soil via frass ecosystem on the brand site).
  • Broker partnership: TLK Fusion provides local retail coverage; White Eagle provides product, education, and approval discipline.
  • Program mindset: Samples → test → measured expansion, not “spray and pray” listings.

Shopper jobs-to-be-done (talk tracks)

  • “I buy mealworms every week—give me a size that lasts.”
  • “My flock goes crazy for bugs; I want a natural treat that feels like foraging.”
  • “I keep 20+ birds / multiple coops—small bags are wasteful.”
  • “I prefer shelf-stable over live insects for convenience.”
