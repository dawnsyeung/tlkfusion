Process tool

Call Script and Discovery Questions

Opening

“White Eagle Nutrition has developed a whole dried BSFL line in 5 lb, 10 lb, and 20 lb value formats. We believe it fills a gap between small insect-treat bags and true high-frequency use by backyard flocks, homesteaders, and farms. I’d like to understand your poultry category and determine whether an online, regional, or chain test makes sense.”

Discovery questions

  1. How important is backyard poultry or homesteading to your current customer base?
  2. Which dried insect products and sizes currently perform best?
  3. Do customers request larger or more economical packages?
  4. Is the category purchased mainly in store, online, or through ship-to-store?
  5. What shelf footprint, package dimensions, and case pack work best?
  6. What opening margin and promotional margin does the category require?
  7. Does the buyer prefer a three-SKU ladder or a focused opening assortment?
  8. Would the account consider a regional, online-only, seasonal, or limited-store test?
  9. What are the new-vendor, compliance, EDI, insurance, and distribution requirements?
  10. What proof of demand or velocity would be required to expand the line?
